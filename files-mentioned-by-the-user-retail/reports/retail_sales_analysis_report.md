# Retail Sales Analysis Report

## Executive Summary

This report analyzes 1,987 retail transactions to understand revenue, profit, category performance, customer behavior, seasonality, and operational patterns.

The business generated total revenue of 908,230, total cost of 486,861.05, and total profit of 421,368.95. The overall profit margin is approximately 46%, which indicates a strong pricing and cost structure. However, the analysis also shows important risks: revenue is highly seasonal, the customer base is small, and revenue growth does not always convert into profit growth.

The strongest opportunities are improving demand for Beauty products, protecting margins in Electronics, increasing average order value in Clothing, and preparing operations for predictable Q4 demand.

## Business Objectives

The analysis was conducted to answer the following questions:

- What drives revenue and profit?
- Which product categories perform best?
- How do customer segments contribute to sales?
- Is business growth sustainable or seasonal?
- Where are the main cost, pricing, and operational inefficiencies?

## Dataset Overview

The dataset contains retail transaction-level records with customer, product, pricing, cost, and sales information.

Key fields include:

- Transaction ID

- Customer ID

## KPI Interpretation

The business is profitable at an overall level, with a strong profit margin of about 46%. The average margin per unit is 84.36, showing that products are being sold with healthy unit economics.

Revenue per customer is also high at 5,859.55. However, the total customer base is only 155 customers. This creates dependency risk because revenue may be concentrated among a relatively small number of buyers.

## Category Performance

| Category | Revenue | Profit | Cost | Margin | Orders |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Beauty | 286,790 | 137,441.80 | 149,348.20 | 47.92% | 611 |
| Clothing | 309,995 | 143,236.30 | 166,758.70 | 46.21% | 698 |
| Electronics | 311,445 | 140,690.85 | 170,754.15 | 45.17% | 678 |

### Beauty

Beauty has the highest margin at 47.92% and the lowest cost base, but it has the lowest order volume.

**Insight:** Beauty is a high-margin but underutilized category. It has strong profitability but limited demand.

**Recommendation:** Increase category visibility through targeted marketing, premium positioning, upselling, and product bundles.

### Clothing

Clothing has the highest profit and the highest number of orders. Its margin is moderate compared with Beauty, but strong volume makes it the most balanced category.

**Insight:** Clothing is the stable volume engine of the business.

**Recommendation:** Improve average order value through bundles, cross-selling, and higher-priced product recommendations.

### Electronics

Electronics has the highest revenue and highest cost, but the lowest margin.

**Insight:** Electronics is a revenue driver, but profitability is limited by its cost structure.

**Recommendation:** Optimize supplier costs, improve product mix, and reduce unnecessary discounting.

## Monthly Sales Trend

Revenue remains relatively stable from January to August, generally ranging between 40K and 58K. A sharp increase occurs from September to December, with revenue reaching up to 141K.

The pattern repeats across years, showing strong and predictable seasonality.

### Growth Highlights

| Period | Revenue Growth |
| --- | ---: |
| September 2022 | +192% |
| September 2023 | +139% |

### Monthly Trend Insights

- Most revenue is generated in Q4.
- The business is heavily seasonal.
- Demand is predictable and recurring.
- Inventory, staffing, and marketing should be planned before September.

## Month-over-Month Performance

Month-over-month analysis shows that revenue growth does not always translate into profit growth.

Examples:

| Period | Revenue Change | Profit Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| October 2022 | +9.9% | -23.6% |
| October 2023 | -14% | -31% |
| January 2023 | -66.9% | -51.7% |

### MoM Insights

- Profit is more sensitive than revenue during some periods.
- Growth is mainly volume-driven rather than margin-driven.
- Post-season revenue drops create business risk.
- April to August represents baseline business performance.

## Hourly Sales Trend

Sales begin increasing from 5 PM onward. Peak performance occurs between 6 PM and 9 PM, with the highest order activity between 7 PM and 8 PM.

### Hourly Trend Insights

- Evening hours drive a large share of daily sales.
- Customer behavior suggests strong post-work shopping activity.
- Staffing, checkout capacity, and stock availability should be strongest during evening hours.

## Customer Analysis by Age Group

| Age Group | Revenue | Profit |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 18-25 | 168,960 | 82,186 |
| 25-45 | 380,535 | 173,125 |
| 45+ | 358,735 | 166,056 |

### Age Group Insights

- Customers aged 25-45 are the primary revenue and profit drivers.
- Customers aged 45+ also contribute strongly and should not be ignored.
- Customers aged 18-25 contribute the least revenue and profit.

### Age Group Recommendations

- Target the 25-45 group for growth campaigns.
- Expand focus on the 45+ segment through relevant offers.
- Offer affordable product options for younger customers.

## Key Findings

1. The business has a strong overall profit margin of about 46%.
2. Q4 dominates revenue, proving that the business is highly seasonal.
3. Revenue growth does not always result in profit growth.
4. Electronics generates high revenue but faces margin pressure from high costs.
5. Beauty has the best margin but needs stronger demand generation.
6. Clothing is the most stable and balanced category.
7. The small customer base creates dependency risk.
8. Evening hours are the most important sales period.

## Business Risks

| Risk | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| Seasonality risk | Revenue depends heavily on Q4 sales. |
| Customer concentration risk | Only 155 customers generate the full revenue base. |
| Margin pressure | Some revenue growth periods show weaker profit growth. |
| Cost-heavy category risk | Electronics has high cost and the lowest margin. |
| Underutilized category risk | Beauty has high margin but low order volume. |

## Recommendations

- Prepare inventory, staffing, and marketing campaigns before September.
- Grow Beauty demand through promotion, bundling, and premium positioning.
- Protect Electronics profitability by negotiating supplier costs and improving product mix.
- Increase Clothing average order value through bundles and cross-selling.
- Target the 25-45 and 45+ age groups with segment-specific campaigns.
- Expand the customer base to reduce dependency on a small number of customers.
- Increase staff and checkout readiness during 6 PM to 9 PM.

## Conclusion

The retail business is profitable and has strong overall margins, but growth is heavily influenced by seasonality and volume. High revenue alone is not enough to measure business health because cost structure, customer mix, category performance, and timing all affect profitability.

The strongest strategic focus should be on increasing high-margin Beauty sales, protecting Electronics margins, growing Clothing order value, and preparing operations for Q4 demand. With better customer targeting and cost control, the business can improve both revenue quality and long-term profitability.
